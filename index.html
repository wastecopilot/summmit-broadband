FAQ

Answers before you switch.

Installation, speeds and switching, explained simply. Still deciding? A fiber specialist can walk you through every option.

Call to order

  • Installation is free. A professional technician connects fiber to your home, sets up your included Wi-Fi router and makes sure your devices are online before they leave. Just call to order and pick an installation time that works for you.

  • Your upload speed matches your download speed. On a 1 Gig plan you get up to 1 Gig in both directions, so video calls, cloud backups, gaming and uploading large files feel just as fast as streaming.

  • 500 Mbps suits 1–3 users with HD streaming and browsing. 1 Gig is ideal for multi-device families with several 4K streams. 2 Gig is built for creators and heavy simultaneous use, and 5 Gig serves multi-gig homes and home businesses in select areas.

  • Summit Fiber plans have no throttling and no contracts. Every plan includes a price lock and a 30-day money-back guarantee.

  • Every fiber plan includes a Wi-Fi router. The 2 Gig and 5 Gig plans also include a Wi-Fi extender, and 5 Gig comes with a Wi-Fi 7 router. On 500 Mbps and 1 Gig, an extender is available for $10/mo. Plume AI-driven Wi-Fi and security features are included at no extra cost.

  • Call to order and schedule your free installation. We recommend keeping your current service active until your Summit Fiber connection is live, then cancelling your previous provider so you are never without internet.

  • Summit Broadband serves communities across Florida, and availability varies by address. Enter your ZIP code above or call to confirm the speeds and services available at your home.

  • Yes. SBB TV, Summit Stream, DIRECTV via Internet and Home Phone can be added to your fiber service at any time and combined on one simple bill.
