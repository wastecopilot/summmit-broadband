Placing your order

When you call to order, our team confirms availability at your address, reviews plan options and pricing with you, and submits your order for provisioning.

Installation

Once your order is accepted, you will receive an installation appointment. A professional technician installs your fiber connection and included equipment. Installation timing depends on address, community and scheduling availability.

Service terms

Services are provided under the service provider’s terms and conditions in effect at the time of order, including any promotional, AutoPay and equipment terms. Eligible services include a 30-day money-back guarantee.
